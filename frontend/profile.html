Transparent scoring formula: Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

RICE Scoring Weights: Reach is estimated quarterly customers; Impact ranges from 0.25 (minimal) to 3 (massive); Confidence ranges from 50% to 100%; Effort is sprint person-weeks. All inputs are labeled as estimates.
